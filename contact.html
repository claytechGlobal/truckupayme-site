FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of freight do you transport?

We specialize in frozen seafood and other refrigerated freight, with temperature control for the whole trip. We also haul general freight.

Where do you provide service?

Regional and long-haul routes throughout the continental United States, depending on load availability.

Are you an owner-operator?

Yes. TRUCKUPAYME INC is owner-operated, so you work directly with the person hauling your load.

Do you work with brokers and 3PLs?

Yes. We haul for seafood distributors, processors, food manufacturers, brokers and 3PL providers.

When and how do I pay?

You are invoiced after delivery for the price on your rate confirmation. You can pay by card or ACH through Stripe's secure checkout.

How do I request a quote?
