Privacy Policy

What information we collect and how we use it.

Effective September 29, 2026

What we collect

We collect the information you give us when you request a quote or book a load: your name, company, phone number, email address, pickup and delivery locations and shipment details.

How we use it

We use your information only to prepare quotes, arrange and carry out transportation, send invoices and receipts, and communicate with you about your shipments. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your card or bank details are entered directly with Stripe and handled under Stripe's privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy). We do not store full card numbers.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to complete your shipment (for example with the pickup or delivery facility), with our payment processor, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing Jamal@truckupayme.com.
