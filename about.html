About us

Driven by reliability. Focused on you.

TRUCKUPAYME INC is a dedicated owner-operator trucking company providing refrigerated freight transportation, with a focus on frozen seafood. We also haul general freight. The business is built on honesty, reliability and a strong work ethic.

Operating from Raleigh, North Carolina, we serve shippers, brokers, distributors and logistics companies on regional and long-haul routes. Because you work directly with the owner, you get one point of contact who is responsible for your load from pickup to final delivery.

  • Owner-operated for direct communication and accountability
  • Temperature control from pickup to delivery
  • Regional and long-haul routes across the United States
  • Clear updates while your freight is in transit

Mission

Provide dependable refrigerated transportation through honesty, professionalism and consistent on-time delivery.

Vision

Become a trusted partner for seafood distributors, food manufacturers, brokers and logistics providers nationwide.

Values

Safety first, honest communication, reliability, professionalism and product integrity on every load.
