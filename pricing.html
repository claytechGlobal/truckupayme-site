Pricing & payment

Every load is priced individually and confirmed in writing, in U.S. dollars, before dispatch. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

What goes into a quote

DistanceLoaded miles from pickup to delivery
TemperatureFrozen or chilled setpoint, and continuous vs. cycle run
Weight & sizeFull truckload, pallet count and total weight
TimingPickup date, delivery deadline and appointment windows
ExtrasAdditional stops, detention or lumper fees, only when agreed in advance

All prices are quoted and billed in U.S. dollars (USD).

How you pay

  • After delivery we send an invoice for the amount on your rate confirmation.
  • Pay online by credit card, debit card or ACH bank transfer through our payment processor, Stripe.
  • Card details are entered on Stripe's secure payment page. TRUCKUPAYME INC never sees or stores your full card number.
  • You receive an email receipt for every payment.
